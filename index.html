About

A developer who cares about craft.

I'm Allen, a full-stack developer based in the Philippines. I build complete, working products — the database schema, the API, the interface, the deployment, and the tests.

Most of my work is in PHP with Laravel and MySQL, and in React and Next.js on the front end. I've shipped inventory and point-of-sale systems, community directory platforms, and games — including one with 192 automated tests. I care about the details that users never see but always notice: FEFO batch tracking, audit logs, WCAG-AA contrast, and RBAC done properly.

I'm always learning, always building, and looking for opportunities to grow alongside great teams and meaningful projects.
